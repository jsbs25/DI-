# EARTH ONE - MASTER PROJECT PLAN

> **"ONE EARTH. ONE WORLD. ONE HUMANITY."**
> 一颗地球，一个世界，一个人类。

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision

**EARTH ONE** is the next-generation global digital ecosystem bridging physical geography, real-time social networking, intercultural dialogue, multilingual communication, digital creators, intellectual games, and advanced AI intelligence.

Unlike traditional siloed social networks, EARTH ONE is anchored by an **interactive 3D Digital Globe**. Every registered user is a shining node on Earth, giving humanity a tangible, shared spatial context.

---

## 2. Platform Architecture Principles

1. **Spatial Grounding**: Geography is the root navigation axis. Users discover content, cultural conversations, and creators by spinning and zooming into Earth.
2. **True Global Presence**: Full support for 6 initial world languages (Chinese, English, Arabic RTL, Spanish, French, Russian) with automatic real-time translation powered by Gemini.
3. **Privacy First**: Exact coordinates are never exposed without explicit consent. Users choose country and city visibility.
4. **Unified Identity**: Single unified account across social posts, instant messaging, creator articles, video streams, multiplayer games, and the AI Hub.
5. **High Aesthetic Fidelity**: Dark cosmos theme, Apple-like minimal elegance, glassmorphic HUD dock, high contrast readability, and silky-smooth Three.js rendering.

---

## 3. Phased Implementation Roadmap

- **Phase 1 (Active)**: Core 3D Interactive Digital Globe, User Authentication & Profiles, Global Nodes, Social Posts Feed (with likes, comments, filters), Multilingual i18n, Global Search.
- **Phase 2 (Active)**: Real-Time Messaging & Direct Conversations, Global Chatrooms, AI Auto-Translation with original toggle.
- **Phase 3 (Active)**: Global Creator Hub (Long-form articles, voluntary tipping, creative collections), Video Streaming Hub.
- **Phase 4 (Active)**: EARTH ONE AI HUB (Multi-model integration, AI translator, writer, summary, tutor).
- **Phase 5 (Active)**: Global Game Center (Multiplayer Chess & Gomoku, Global Trivia Challenge, country matchups), Glassmorphic Ad Network.
- **Phase 6**: Enterprise deployment scaling, security audits, automated testing.
